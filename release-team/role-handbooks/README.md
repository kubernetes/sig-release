# Role Handbooks

These handbooks are maintained by current and previous contributors who have
staffed these roles. They are intended to be living documents that evolve as
the roles and project evolve. Do not treat them as rules set in stone, but as
guidelines to be re-examined.

The [Release Team README](../README.md#kubernetes-release-team-roles) describes
how the roles fit together. The handbooks are:

- [Release Team Lead](release-team-lead/README.md)
- [Emeritus Adviser](emeritus-adviser/README.md)
- [Enhancements](enhancements/README.md)
- [Release Signal](release-signal/README.md)
- [Docs](docs/README.md)
- [Communications](communications/README.md)

Guidance that applies to every role lives in the
[Release Team onboarding guide](../release-team-onboarding.md) and the
[shadows guide](../shadows.md).
